# Emergent Gravity from Quantum Information: A Comprehensive Review

## Main Topic
This research note synthesizes the key ideas surrounding the concept of emergent gravity, which posits that the familiar phenomena of spacetime geometry and gravity arise from underlying structures of quantum information and entanglement. It explores the mathematical and physical frameworks that support this perspective, including the AdS/CFT correspondence, tensor network models, and holographic principles.

## Core Claims
- Emergent gravity suggests that spacetime and gravity are not fundamental features of the universe, but rather properties that emerge from more basic quantum information structures.
- Quantum entanglement entropy is intimately connected with the geometric properties of spacetime, suggesting a deep relationship between information and gravity.
- The AdS/CFT correspondence, tensor networks, and the holographic principle provide concrete realizations of how geometric properties can arise from quantum information patterns.
- Information-theoretic measures, such as entanglement entropy, provide a mathematical language for understanding gravity as a thermodynamic consequence of quantum correlations.

## Important Concepts
- Entanglement entropy: A measure of the quantum information shared between two subsystems.
- Ryu-Takayanagi formula: Establishes a direct relationship between entanglement entropy in a boundary conformal field theory (CFT) and the geometric area in the bulk anti-de Sitter (AdS) space.
- Tensor networks: Models that demonstrate how geometry can emerge from patterns of entanglement between quantum systems.
- ER=EPR conjecture: Suggests that geometric connections in spacetime correspond to quantum entanglement between regions.
- Holographic principle: The information content of any region of space can be encoded on its boundary surface.

## Assumptions
- The fundamental nature of the universe is quantum mechanical, and classical properties like spacetime and gravity are emergent.
- Quantum entanglement is a key ingredient in the structure of spacetime and the dynamics of gravity.
- The AdS/CFT correspondence and related holographic principles are valid and applicable to our universe.

## Open Questions
- How do emergent gravity mechanisms reconcile with the fundamental incompatibility between general relativity and quantum mechanics?
- What is the precise nature of the relationship between quantum entanglement and spacetime geometry?
- How do these ideas apply to the information paradox in black hole physics?
- What are the future directions in quantum gravity research?
